  <i>❝ I hate nice girls.
Just greeting each other can make me curious, just sending messages to each other makes me anxious.
If they called me, I would always look at my call list with a stupid smile.
But I know. They are just being nice.
If they are good to me, it means they are also good to others. I almost forgot about that.
If reality is cruel, then lies are good.
So, kindness is a lie.
I always held this hope. And I always misunderstand (this).
Until I didn't realize it, I had stopped hoping.
Experienced loners won't fall into the same trap.
As a veteran of life's battles, I am the strongest when it comes to defeat.
So forever, I hate good girls. ❞</i>
